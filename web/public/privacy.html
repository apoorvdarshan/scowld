Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

1. Overview

Scowld is a free, open-source iOS AI companion app. Conversations run live, speech to speech, with the provider you choose (Google Gemini Live, OpenAI Realtime, or xAI Grok Voice), using your own API key. The provider hears your voice directly and answers in its own voice. Scowld 2.0.1 and earlier instead use separate AI chat, speech-to-text, and text-to-speech providers that you choose (see AI and Voice Processing). API keys you enter are stored in the iOS Keychain on your device and are sent only to that provider. Scowld also includes an optional hands-free wake mode for starting a live conversation by saying the selected companion name.

This policy explains what information is processed when you use the app and website.

2. Account and App Analytics

The iOS app does not require a Scowld account. The app does not include advertising SDKs or third-party app analytics SDKs. It includes the RevenueCat SDK only to process optional tips (see Optional Tips below).

Scowld 2.0.1 and earlier also include a third-party avatar component, the bundled Amica page, that was built with Sentry error reporting set up by its upstream project. In those versions it can send error reports, performance data, and session replays of the avatar view to Sentry. Scowld does not receive or use this data, and the next Scowld update removes this reporting.

  • We do not ask for your name, email, location, or account password in the iOS app.
  • We do not sell personal data.
  • We do not use advertising frameworks in the iOS app.
  • The marketing website is hosted on Cloudflare Workers and does not include a third-party analytics component.

3. AI and Voice Processing

When you start a live conversation, Scowld opens one live connection from your device to the provider you selected. While the conversation is active, Scowld sends the following directly to that provider:

  • Your microphone audio, streamed continuously for as long as the conversation is active, not only while you speak.
  • The companion's instructions (her name, the system prompt, and the reply language) and recent messages from the active chat as context.
  • Camera frames while the camera is on, with Google Gemini Live or OpenAI Realtime (see Camera Access).

The provider answers with speech in the voice you picked and with transcripts of what you said and what it said. Scowld plays her voice, shows captions if you turn them on, and saves each turn's transcript in the active chat on your device. With OpenAI Realtime, what you say is transcribed inside the same session (with OpenAI's whisper-1 model) so it can be saved to the chat. Providers process this data under their own terms and privacy policies.

A live conversation ends by itself after 90 seconds of silence, and when you leave the Chat tab or the app goes to the background. Ending a conversation closes the connection and stops sending microphone audio to the provider.

Scowld does not operate a hosted AI or speech proxy for app conversations. Hands-free wake detection runs on device, and nothing is sent to a provider before a live conversation starts.

Scowld 2.0.1 and earlier work differently: voice input is transcribed by native iOS speech recognition or the cloud speech-to-text provider you choose, the text and selected conversation context are sent to the AI chat provider you choose, and replies are spoken by ElevenLabs or OpenAI text-to-speech. In those versions, a camera frame is sent only with a message you send while the camera is on, and voice samples in Settings play from files bundled with the app without calling ElevenLabs. The next Scowld update replaces this with the live conversations described above.

4. Camera Access

Scowld requests camera access for the optional vision feature. The camera is off until you turn it on. While it is on during a live conversation:

  • With Google Gemini Live, Scowld sends a camera frame about every 2 seconds.
  • With OpenAI Realtime, Scowld sends the latest frame with each thing you say.
  • xAI Grok Voice does not support the camera, so no frames are sent.
  • Frames are captured on device and sent only to the selected provider. Scowld does not record or store camera data, and does not save images to your photo library.
  • Camera access can be disabled from the app or iOS settings.
  • When the camera is off, Scowld does not capture camera frames.

Scowld does not use Apple's TrueDepth API or ARKit face tracking. Scowld does not collect, use, store, disclose, share, or retain face geometry, depth maps, facial blend shapes, facial expressions, biometric identifiers, or any other face data.

5. Face Data and TrueDepth API

Scowld does not use Apple's TrueDepth API. The app does not collect, use, store, disclose, share, or retain face geometry, depth maps, facial blend shapes, facial expressions, biometric identifiers, or any other face data.

  • No TrueDepth information is collected by Scowld.
  • No TrueDepth or face data is used by Scowld for any purpose.
  • No TrueDepth or face data is stored locally by Scowld or on Scowld servers.
  • No TrueDepth or face data is disclosed or shared with AI, speech, hosting, Apple, or any other third party by Scowld.
  • No TrueDepth or face data is retained because Scowld does not collect it.

6. Microphone Access

Scowld requests microphone access for live conversations and optional hands-free wake mode, and speech recognition permission only for hands-free wake mode.

  • While a live conversation is active, microphone audio is streamed continuously to the selected provider, which hears it directly and produces the transcripts.
  • When hands-free mode is enabled, Scowld may keep the microphone active while the app is open and idle to listen on device, with Apple's on-device speech recognition, only for Bella or your saved custom companion name. This audio is not sent to any provider.
  • After the wake name is detected, Scowld starts a live conversation, and from then on microphone audio is streamed to the selected provider.
  • A live conversation can be ended with the stop button, hands-free mode can be turned off in the composer, and microphone permission can be revoked from iOS settings.
  • In Scowld 2.0.1 and earlier, recorded voice input is transcribed with native iOS speech recognition or the cloud speech-to-text provider you configure, and the recognized text is sent to the selected AI provider.

7. Third-Party Providers

Scowld can integrate with third-party providers you configure. These providers may process data needed to provide app features:

  • Live voice providers: Google (Gemini Live), OpenAI (Realtime), and xAI (Grok Voice). The provider you select receives your microphone audio, conversation context, and camera frames as described above.
  • Earlier versions: Scowld 2.0.1 and earlier send data to the separate AI chat, speech-to-text, and text-to-speech providers you configure in those versions.
  • Apple for iOS platform services such as Keychain, on-device speech recognition, camera, microphone, and app distribution.
  • RevenueCat to validate and record optional tips (in-app purchases).
  • Cloudflare for website deployment and delivery.

These third-party services have their own privacy policies and terms. You are responsible for the provider accounts and API keys you configure.

8. Optional Tips

Scowld offers optional one-time tips in About > Support Scowld. Tips do not unlock any features, and Scowld works the same without them.

  • Payments are processed by Apple through the App Store. Scowld never receives your payment card details, name, or Apple Account email.
  • The app uses RevenueCat, a purchase management service, to validate and record tips. RevenueCat receives an anonymous identifier generated on your device, purchase details (the tip, price, currency, storefront, and date), basic device and app information (such as iOS version, app version, device model, and locale), and the IP address of its network requests.
  • This information is used to complete and verify purchases and to report tip revenue. Scowld does not link it to your identity or use it for advertising.
  • RevenueCat processes this data under its own privacy policy.

9. Provider API Keys

Scowld lets you enter your own provider API keys. Provider keys are stored in the iOS Keychain on your device, are sent only to the provider they belong to, and are not included in the App Store binary.

  • Provider keys are not stored in UserDefaults or bundled files.
  • When you update from Scowld 2.0.1 or earlier, keys saved for providers the app no longer uses are deleted from the Keychain. Saved Gemini, OpenAI, and xAI keys keep working.
  • Provider keys are not committed to the source repository.
  • Delete the app or remove keys from Settings to stop using saved keys.

10. Local Device Storage

Scowld stores app data locally on your device using Apple's local storage frameworks. This may include:

  • Past chats: the text transcript of each turn, what you said (or typed, in Scowld 2.0.1 and earlier) and what she said.
  • The selected active chat.
  • Character settings such as avatar, custom name, and system prompt.
  • Voice, language, caption, and hands-free wake preferences.
  • Provider choices, model choices, and local Keychain references.

Deleting the app removes local app data from the device, subject to normal iOS behavior and backups.

11. Children's Privacy

Scowld is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
