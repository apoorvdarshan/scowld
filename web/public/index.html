Talk to Bella.

Scowld is a free, open-source AI voice companion for iPhone and iPad. She listens, answers out loud and can look at what you show her, using AI keys you bring yourself.

Bella

Hi, I'm Bella. Turn on hands-free, say my name, and I'll start listening. I answer out loud, and typing works too.

  • Download on the App Store
  • Read the source on GitHub

Each look is one of the three characters in the app. Bella is her default name, and you can give her your own.

What she can do

Voice in, voice out, with a character who reacts while she talks.

Talk live
Tap the mic and talk naturally. The provider you choose hears you directly and answers out loud in its own voice.
Hands-free wake
When it's on, Scowld listens on device for “Bella” or the name you choose, then starts a live conversation.
Interrupt anytime
Start talking while she speaks and she stops to listen. You can also type, and she answers out loud.
A character who reacts
A 3D VRM character with idle motion and lip sync that follows her live voice.
Optional vision
Turn on the camera when you want her to respond to what you're looking at. It's off by default and works with Gemini and OpenAI.
Past chats
Conversations are saved on your device. Switch between them, or reuse one as context for new replies.
Quick tips at home
A short in-app guide explains the wake phrase and the hands-free, live voice, eye and send controls.
iPhone and iPad
Runs on iOS 17 or later. There's no account to create.

Questions

What is Scowld?

Scowld is a free, open-source iOS AI voice companion with an animated on-screen avatar. You talk with her live by voice, or type, and she answers out loud in the voice of the provider you choose. With Gemini or OpenAI, she can also use the camera to respond to what you show her. You bring your own API key for Google Gemini Live, OpenAI Realtime, or xAI Grok Voice.

Is Scowld free?

Yes. Scowld is free and open source (MIT). There are no subscriptions, paywalls, or voice credits. Optional one-time tips in the app support development and unlock nothing. You bring your own provider API keys and pay your chosen providers directly for usage.

Can Scowld see through the camera?

Yes, optionally, with Google Gemini Live or OpenAI Realtime. While the camera is on, Scowld sends frames to your provider so she can respond to what you show her, like an object you hold up. Gemini gets a frame about every 2 seconds, and OpenAI gets the latest frame with each thing you say or type. The camera is off by default, and xAI Grok Voice doesn't support it.

Does Scowld work on iPhone and iPad?

Yes. Scowld runs on iPhone and iPad with iOS 17 or later. Settings, provider keys, and saved chats stay on the device.

How is Scowld different from other AI companion apps?

Scowld is bring-your-own-key and open source, so there is no paywall and no server that stores your keys or conversations — requests go straight from your device to the providers you pick. It also pairs an animated avatar with live voice conversations you can interrupt, optional camera vision, hands-free wake, and a choice of three live voice providers.

What does bring-your-own-key (BYOK) mean?

You use your own API key from the live voice provider you choose: Google, OpenAI, or xAI. Keys are stored only in the iOS Keychain on your device and each one is sent only to its own provider, so Scowld never runs a server that sees or stores them.

Which AI providers does Scowld support?

Three live voice providers: Google Gemini Live, OpenAI Realtime, and xAI Grok Voice. Each one hears you directly and answers in its own voice. You can switch providers, models, and voices any time in Settings.

Does Scowld use separate speech-to-text and text-to-speech?

No. Conversations are live speech-to-speech: the provider hears your voice directly and answers in one of its built-in voices, so there is no separate transcription or voice service to set up. Gemini Live has 30 voices, OpenAI Realtime has 10, including Marin and Cedar, and xAI Grok Voice has 5: Ara, Eve, Sal, Rex, and Leo.

Are my API keys stored securely?

Yes. Provider keys are stored in the iOS Keychain on your device, are not bundled in the app binary, and are never sent to Scowld servers. Requests go directly from your device to the provider you configured.

Is Scowld open source?

Yes. Scowld is released under the MIT License and the source is available on GitHub at github.com/apoorvdarshan/scowld.

Does Scowld need an account or an internet connection?

No account is required. Live conversations need an internet connection and a valid key for your provider. Hands-free wake listening for her name runs on device.

What is hands-free wake mode?

An optional mode where Scowld listens on device for "Bella" or your custom companion name, using Apple's on-device speech recognition, and then starts a live conversation. It listens only for the name, and nothing is sent to a provider before the conversation starts.
