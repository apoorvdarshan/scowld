Talk to Bella.

Scowld is a free, open-source AI voice companion for iPhone and iPad. She listens, answers out loud and can look at what you show her, using AI keys you bring yourself.

Bella

Hi, I'm Bella. Turn on hands-free, say my name, and I'll start listening. I answer out loud, and typing works too.

Each look is one of the three characters in the app. Bella is her default name, and you can give her your own.

What she can do

Voice in, voice out, with a character who reacts while she talks.

Talk out loud
Speak from the composer with native iOS speech recognition or a cloud speech-to-text provider.
Hands-free wake
When it's on, Scowld listens on device for “Bella” or the name you choose, then starts recording.
Spoken replies
Answers are read aloud by ElevenLabs or OpenAI voices, and you can preview ElevenLabs voices before choosing.
A character who reacts
A 3D VRM character with lip sync, idle motion and expressions that follow the reply.
Optional vision
Turn on the camera when you want her to respond to what you're looking at. It's off by default.
Past chats
Conversations are saved on your device. Switch between them, or reuse one as context for new replies.
Quick tips at home
A short in-app guide explains the wake phrase and the eye, hands-free, voice, send and cancel controls.
iPhone and iPad
Runs on iOS 17 or later. There's no account to create.

Questions

What is Scowld?

Scowld is a free, open-source iOS AI voice companion with an animated on-screen avatar. You talk by voice or text, get spoken replies through ElevenLabs or OpenAI voices, and can optionally let it use the camera so it responds to what you show it. You bring your own AI, speech-to-text, and text-to-speech API keys.

Is Scowld free?

Yes. Scowld is free and open source (MIT). There are no subscriptions, paywalls, or voice credits. Optional one-time tips in the app support development and unlock nothing. You bring your own provider API keys and pay your chosen providers directly for usage.

Can Scowld see through the camera?

Yes, optionally. When you enable the camera, Scowld can send a frame to your chosen AI provider so the companion responds to what you show it — like holding up an object. Camera vision is off by default and only used when you send a message with it enabled.

Does Scowld work on iPhone and iPad?

Yes. Scowld runs on iPhone and iPad with iOS 17 or later. Settings, provider keys, and saved chats stay on the device.

How is Scowld different from other AI companion apps?

Scowld is bring-your-own-key and open source, so there is no paywall and no server that stores your keys or conversations — requests go straight from your device to the providers you pick. It also pairs an animated avatar with optional camera vision, hands-free wake, and swappable AI, speech-to-text, and text-to-speech providers.

What does bring-your-own-key (BYOK) mean?

You use your own API keys for the AI, speech-to-text, and text-to-speech providers you choose. Keys are stored only in the iOS Keychain on your device, so Scowld never runs a server that sees or stores them.

Which AI providers does Scowld support?

Gemini, OpenAI, Claude, Ollama, Groq, OpenRouter, xAI, Together AI, Hugging Face, Venice AI, and Moonshot AI. You can switch providers and models any time in Settings.

What speech-to-text and text-to-speech options are available?

Voice input works with native iOS speech recognition or cloud providers like OpenAI, Groq Whisper, Deepgram, AssemblyAI, and Google Cloud Speech-to-Text. Spoken replies use ElevenLabs or OpenAI voices.

Are my API keys stored securely?

Yes. Provider keys are stored in the iOS Keychain on your device, are not bundled in the app binary, and are never sent to Scowld servers. Requests go directly from your device to the provider you configured.

Is Scowld open source?

Yes. Scowld is released under the MIT License and the source is available on GitHub at github.com/apoorvdarshan/scowld.

Does Scowld need an account or an internet connection?

No account is required. Cloud AI, speech-to-text, and text-to-speech need an internet connection and valid provider keys, while native iOS speech recognition works on device.

What is hands-free wake mode?

An optional mode where Scowld listens on device for "Bella" or your custom companion name to start voice recording. Wake detection is processed on device and is not sent to providers before recording starts.
