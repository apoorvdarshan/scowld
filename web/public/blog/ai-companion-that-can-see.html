An AI Companion That Can See What You Show It

Published July 9, 2026. Updated October 16, 2026. 4 minute read.

Text is a narrow way to talk to an AI. You describe a thing in words, the model imagines it, and something is always lost in translation. Scowld takes a different route: with the camera enabled, the companion can respond to what you physically show it — hold up an object, point at something on your desk, or just let it see the room.

This vision feature is optional and off by default. When you turn it on, it turns a one-way chat into something closer to showing a friend what you're looking at.

How the camera vision works

While the camera is on during a live conversation, Scowld captures frames from the front camera and sends them to the provider you configured: about one every 2 seconds with Google Gemini Live, or the latest frame with each thing you say with OpenAI Realtime. With Scowld Premium, at most one frame every 2 seconds goes through Scowld's server to Google Gemini Live. The model sees the image and the companion replies out loud.

  • Frames are captured on device and only sent while the camera is on.
  • Vision works with Google Gemini Live, OpenAI Realtime, and Scowld Premium. xAI Grok Voice doesn't support the camera.
  • Images are never saved to your photo library by Scowld.

Privacy by design

Vision is opt-in and off by default. Frames are sent only while the camera is on, and Scowld doesn't record or store camera data. With your own key, no Scowld server sees your images — frames go straight from your device to the provider whose key you added. With Scowld Premium, frames are relayed through Scowld's server to Google Gemini Live and aren't stored there.

Scowld also does not use Apple's TrueDepth API or ARKit face tracking, and it does not collect, store, or share face geometry, depth maps, facial expressions, or any other face data. The camera is there to see the world you point it at, not to profile you.

When showing beats telling

  • Ask what something is when you don't have the words for it.
  • Get a second opinion on an outfit, a label, or a screen.
  • Talk through something on your desk without typing a description.

How to try it

Download Scowld from the App Store, then use Scowld Premium or add a Google Gemini or OpenAI key in Settings > Live Voice. Turn on the camera with the eye button and talk to her. With your own key, you only pay your provider for what you use — there's no subscription. Scowld Premium is an optional paid alternative that needs no key.

Frequently asked questions

Does Scowld record video?

No. While the camera is on, Scowld sends still frames (about one every 2 seconds with Gemini, one with each thing you say with OpenAI, or at most one every 2 seconds with Scowld Premium), but it doesn't record or store camera data, and images are not saved to your photo library.

Which providers support vision in Scowld?

Vision works with Google Gemini Live, OpenAI Realtime, and Scowld Premium, which uses Gemini Live. xAI Grok Voice doesn't support the camera. Choose Scowld Premium, or a provider with your own key, in Settings > Live Voice.
