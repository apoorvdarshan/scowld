What 'Bring Your Own Key' Means for an AI App

Published July 9, 2026. Updated October 16, 2026. 5 minute read.

"Bring your own key" (BYOK) means an app doesn't sell you AI usage — instead, you bring an API key from a provider you already trust, and the app talks to that provider directly on your behalf. Scowld is built this way from the ground up.

The practical result: with your own key, there's no Scowld subscription, no paywall, and no middle-man server holding your conversations or your keys. Scowld also offers an optional paid alternative, Scowld Premium, for people who'd rather not get a key (more on that below).

Why BYOK matters

  • Privacy: with your own key, requests go from your device straight to the provider you configured. No Scowld server receives, relays, or stores your keys or chats.
  • Cost: you pay your chosen provider for what you use instead of a flat monthly fee. Live voice is billed by the amount of audio, so light use costs little and long conversations cost more.
  • Control: switch providers and models whenever you want, and stop instantly by removing the key.

Where your keys are stored

Keys you enter live in the iOS Keychain on your device. They are not bundled in the app binary, not written to plain preferences, and not committed to the open-source repository. Delete the app or remove a key in Settings and it's gone.

Which providers Scowld supports

Scowld talks with you live, speech to speech. The provider you pick hears you directly and answers in its own voice, so one key covers the whole conversation:

  • Google Gemini Live: 14 built-in female voices, plus camera vision.
  • OpenAI Realtime: 4 female voices (Marin, Shimmer, Coral, and Sage), plus camera vision.
  • xAI Grok Voice: 2 female voices (Ara and Eve), without camera vision.

Getting started

Download Scowld, open Settings > Live Voice, choose Own API Key, pick a live voice provider and a voice, and paste your key. That's the whole setup — no account, no card on file with Scowld. Scowld is also open source under the MIT License, so you can read exactly how your keys are handled.

If you'd rather not bring a key

BYOK stays free and unlimited in Scowld. If you don't want to set up a provider account, Scowld Premium is an optional alternative: it uses Google Gemini Live with Scowld's own key, relayed in real time through Scowld's server, which doesn't store your conversations. It's a weekly or yearly subscription with a set number of minutes, and you can add minute packs; the FAQ and the terms have the details. With your own key, nothing goes through Scowld's server.

Frequently asked questions

Is a bring-your-own-key app actually free?

With your own key, the Scowld app is free and open source with no subscription. You only pay the live voice provider you choose, based on your own usage. Scowld Premium is an optional paid alternative for talking without a key.

Can Scowld see my API keys?

No. Keys are stored in the iOS Keychain on your device, and conversations that use your key go directly to the provider. There is no Scowld server that receives or stores your keys.
